Testing mean differences
- mu (μ) = population mean of a test score
	- arithmetic average of scores from normative population
- sigma (σ) = the spread of scores
	- how far individual scores typically vary from the average
- alpha = probability of making a type 1 error: falsely rejecting the null hypothesis - saying there is a real effect when there isnt
	- typically set at .05 or .01 (5/1%)
	- the resulting Zcrit score -from a two-tailed test (ex. +-1.96) indicates only a 2.5% chance youll get a mean that large or small (2.5 small, 2.5 large = 5%)
- sigma x bar = standard error
	- take sigma and divide it by the sqr of n
